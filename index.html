A FEW THINGS, BEFORE YOU TEE OFF

Good to know.

What is QGolf membership?+

QGolf offers member rates at selected golf courses across Indonesia. Choose from Premium, Easy, Corporate, and Red membership, with benefits and playing conditions specific to each card.

How do I reserve a round?+

Use the official QAccess app or contact QGolf reservations on 08118-74653. This prototype helps you prepare a round plan; availability and reservations are confirmed by QGolf.

Which membership is right for me?+

Premium offers weekday and weekend access, while Easy focuses on weekday play and weekend afternoons. Corporate is designed for business use. QGolf also offers Premium variants for Junior, Millennial, Ladies, and Elder golfers. Check current eligibility and conditions with the team.

Do I need a physical membership card?+

QGolf memberships are integrated into the official QAccess app. Your digital card goes wherever your game takes you.

More than

QGolf began in April 2013 as PT Qualitas Andalan Bersama, founded by Noldi Noviandie and his partners with a simple ambition: make golf more convenient and rewarding for members and partners.

Today, that spirit lives in its membership programme, selected courses, and community events. A better way to make golf part of your everyday life.
